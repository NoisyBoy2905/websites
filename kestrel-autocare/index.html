Questions

Can I wait while you do the MOT?

Yes. It takes about 45 minutes, and there's a waiting room with Wi-Fi and a coffee machine.

Will you do extra work without asking?

Never. You get a photo and a price for each job, and I only start once you've said yes.

What if my car fails its MOT?

I'll show you what failed and give you a price to fix it. If you leave it with me to repair, the retest is free.

Do you work on hybrids and electric cars?

Yes, for servicing, brakes, tyres, suspension and MOTs. Battery and high-voltage repairs go to a specialist, and I'll tell you who.

Do you do business accounts?

Yes. I look after vans and cars for local firms, with MOT reminders and one invoice a month.
