Good to know

Do I need a patch test before colour?

Yes, if you're new to us or it's been six months since your last colour. Pop in at least 48 hours before your appointment. It takes two minutes and it's free.

What if I need to cancel?

Just give us 24 hours' notice by phone or text. Things happen, so we won't charge for the first late cancellation.

Can I bring a photo?

Please do. Bring a few, and one you don't like as well. It helps more than you'd think.

Is there parking?

Yes, free on the road outside. There's also a step-free entrance and a chair that lowers for wheelchair users.
