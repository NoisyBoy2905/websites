Common questions

Is the drone survey really free?

Yes. It takes about 20 minutes, and the photos are yours to keep even if you don't use us.

Do I need scaffolding?

For most repairs, no. We use roof ladders and a tower where it's safe. New roofs and chimney work need scaffold, and it's always in the quote.

How long does a new roof take?

A 3-bed semi usually takes five to seven working days, depending on the weather. You can stay in the house the whole time.

What does the guarantee cover?

New roofs get 10 years on our work. Flat roofs also come with the maker's guarantee on the materials. Repairs are covered for 12 months.

Do you work in the rain?

Not on open roofs. If rain is due mid-job, we sheet everything over at the end of each day so nothing gets wet.
