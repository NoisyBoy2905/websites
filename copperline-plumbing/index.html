Questions

How often should my boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most boiler warranties need it, and it's when I catch small faults before they turn into a cold house in winter.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

If your boiler is under 10 years old, a repair is usually worth it. If it's older and breaking down every winter, a new one often pays for itself in gas. I'll give you both prices and let you decide.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Turn off the water at the stopcock, usually under the kitchen sink. Switch off the electrics if water is near sockets or lights. Then ring me.

Do you charge to come and quote?

No. Quotes are free. You only pay a callout fee if I'm coming to fix something.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer or cash when the job's done. For new boilers I take a deposit to order the boiler, and the rest on the day it's fitted.
